# PowerBI Service Account Setup Guide

## Overview

PowerBI Service is Microsoft's cloud-based business analytics platform where you'll publish and share your reports for Assignment 2.

**Cost**: FREE (with limitations suitable for this assignment)
**Time Required**: 5-10 minutes
**What You'll Get**: Online workspace to publish 4 reports

---

## Prerequisites

- Email address (personal or university)
- Web browser
- Internet connection

---

## Step-by-Step Signup

### Method 1: Personal Microsoft Account (Recommended for Students)

#### Step 1: Go to PowerBI Website
Visit: **https://app.powerbi.com/**

#### Step 2: Click Sign Up
- Click **"Sign up free"** or **"Try free"** button
- Or click **"Start free"** in the top right

#### Step 3: Enter Email
Two options:

**Option A: Use existing Microsoft account**
- Enter your Microsoft email (@outlook.com, @hotmail.com, @live.com)
- Click Next
- Sign in with existing credentials
- Skip to Step 5

**Option B: Create new account**
- Enter any email address
- Click Next
- Follow prompts to create Microsoft account
- Verify email if required

#### Step 4: Complete Profile
- Enter your name
- Enter phone number (optional)
- Select country/region
- Accept terms and conditions

#### Step 5: Verify Email (if required)
- Check your inbox for verification email
- Click verification link
- Return to PowerBI Service

#### Step 6: Access Workspace
- You're now in PowerBI Service!
- You'll see "My Workspace" automatically created
- This is where you'll publish your reports

---

### Method 2: University Email (@sliit.lk)

Many universities have PowerBI licenses available.

#### Step 1: Check University Access
Visit: **https://app.powerbi.com/**

#### Step 2: Sign in with University Email
- Enter your @sliit.lk email
- Click Next
- You may be redirected to university login portal
- Sign in with university credentials

#### Step 3: Accept License
- If university has license, you'll get Pro features
- Accept terms and continue

#### Step 4: Access Workspace
- You're in!
- May have access to shared workspaces

---

### Method 3: PowerBI Pro Trial (60 days free)

For advanced features (optional, not required for assignment).

#### Steps:
1. Sign up with Method 1
2. Once in PowerBI Service
3. Click your profile icon (top right)
4. Select **"Start trial"** for PowerBI Pro
5. Follow prompts to activate 60-day trial
6. Get additional features (sharing, larger datasets)

## After Signup - Workspace Setup

### Your Workspace
When you first login, you'll see **"My Workspace"**

This is your personal space where you can:
- Upload PowerBI reports (.pbix files)
- View published reports
- Share reports (with Pro)
- Create dashboards

### Creating Additional Workspaces (Optional)

For organization, you can create assignment-specific workspace:

1. Click **Workspaces** in left navigation
2. Click **Create workspace**
3. Name it: "DWBI Assignment 2"
4. Add description
5. Click Save

## Publishing Reports to PowerBI Service

Once you have reports in PowerBI Desktop:

### From PowerBI Desktop:
1. Open your .pbix report
2. Click **Home** tab → **Publish** button
3. Sign in to PowerBI Service (if not already)
4. Select workspace (e.g., "My Workspace")
5. Click **Select**
6. Wait for upload (shows progress)
7. Click **Open '[ReportName]' in Power BI** link
8. Report opens in web browser

### View Published Report:
1. Go to https://app.powerbi.com/
2. Click **Workspaces** → Your workspace
3. See your report listed under **Content**
4. Click report name to open
5. Use this URL for demonstration

## Sharing Reports (For Demonstration)

### Public Sharing (if needed):
Some assignment demonstrations may require public access.

**Enable Public Sharing**:
1. Open report in PowerBI Service
2. Click **File** → **Embed report** → **Publish to web**
3. Click **Create embed code**
4. Copy public URL
5. Anyone with link can view (no signin required)

**⚠️ Warning**: Only use for assignment demonstration, don't share sensitive data publicly.

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Email not accepted
**Solution**: Try different email provider (Outlook, Gmail, etc.)

### Issue: Can't access university account
**Solution**: Contact IT department or use personal Microsoft account

### Issue: Report publish fails
**Solution**: 
- Check file size (<1 GB for free)
- Ensure report saved in PowerBI Desktop
- Try publishing to "My Workspace"
- Check internet connection

### Issue: Can't view published report
**Solution**:
- Refresh browser
- Check correct workspace selected
- Ensure publish completed successfully
